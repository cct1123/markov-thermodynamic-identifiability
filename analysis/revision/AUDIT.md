## Critical findings and repairs

- No substantive contradiction was found in the preserved balanced global proof. Its disconnected-support enumeration, finite-triangle reduction, positivity domains, strict envelope, unique equality and separate endpoint existence remain essential. The initial first-order threshold and numerical non-discovery remain rejected evidence, as in the historical records.
- The exact inverse remains stronger than an algebraic generic-identifiability claim: local raw-rate regularity survives a tree. The numerical relative condition number and entropy derivative can diverge simultaneously. The rewritten paper retains this distinction rather than indiscriminately calling all sparse inversion ill conditioned.
- A known relation k=exp(-1/e²) would permit entropy inference through e and exclude the hard testing alternative. The new testing proposition instead allows k to vary independently at fixed e. Comparing only to the limiting tree would not establish the exponential horizon bound.
- A fold of active positivity constraints is not a saddle-node of two isolated realizations. A complete subcritical realization has an open similarity continuum, and the source exists on either side. A particular transverse perturbation is now proved locally, with broader genericity left open.
- The entropy-gap neighborhood is narrower in its quantified claim than the exact geometric rectangle. Continuity plus the original certified gap proves existence of a neighborhood; sampled off-balanced gaps do not certify a numerical radius.
- Fixed count, state cap, minimum linear order and minimum positive order are explicitly distinguished. The equality of the latter orders is used only for the displayed family.
- A recorded resolved microscopic jump still resets its target after prior missed jumps. Missed events alter the kernel; detector memory or ambiguous labels can additionally break the assumed reset model. The final review corrected the broader initial wording.
- Finite timing/statistics claims cite Fritz–Ertel–Seifert; spatial-resolution claims cite Yu–Harunari separately. Sixteen additions supplement, rather than overwrite, the original bibliography. [Source comparison and access limitations](LITERATURE.md).
