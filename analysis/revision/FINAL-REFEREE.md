## Corrections communicated to the coordinator

1. **Missed-event/reset wording.** The Discussion currently says that a missed transition can make the next recorded event fail to reset the hidden state. If the next recorded mark remains an accurately resolved microscopic transition `i -> j`, its post-event physical state is still `j`, regardless of prior missed events. Memoryless thinning can preserve a reset kernel with changed marking/killing. Detector memory or dead time, uncertain event identity, and blurred labels can invalidate the declared recorded-process model. Suggested replacement: “Missed events change the observed waiting kernel; detector memory, blurred labels, or uncertain event identity can also invalidate the reset model.” The preceding measurement-limitations subsection already makes the needed distinctions more accurately.

2. **Separate the two finite-observation citations.** The literature-comparison sentence jointly cites Fritz–Ertel–Seifert (2025) and Yu–Harunari (2024) for finite statistics, blurred labels, and temporal binning. The former directly covers those topics; the latter studies the spatial scale of coarse graining. Suggested replacement: “Recent work compares waiting-time and current estimators under finite statistics, blurred labels and temporal binning [Fritz2025], and studies how spatial coarse graining changes hidden-dissipation estimates [Yu2024].” This avoids making one citation support an experiment it does not analyze.

## Checks of the new mathematical material

**Covered graph.** Selecting one arrival row and one departure column for every state gives `F(lambda)=(lambda I-T)^(-1) D`. Consequently `F(lambda_2)^(-1)-F(lambda_1)^(-1)=(lambda_2-lambda_1)D^(-1)`, and `T=lambda_1 I-D F(lambda_1)^(-1)`. The multiplication order is correct for a row generator. Additional observed columns recover all remaining mark rates. Nonzero determinants at the source justify the local rational inverse without a shared rate cap. A single hidden reciprocal pair does not imply ambiguity when its endpoints are covered. Opening an absent **unobserved** pair in a covered tree yields entropy discontinuity despite exact generator identification; the stated graph example correctly disclaims fixed trace without compensating slack.

**Rare-event lower bound.** The stationary-weight quotient-rule bound is valid on the stated domain: `Z>=3` and `||d pi/dk||_TV <= (3-2k)/Z <=1`. Changing `k` changes one outgoing rate in each of two rows, so the common/excess-clock separation hazard is at most `k-k^p`. Coupling initial stationary states adds at most that same amount to path TV. The testing inequality and the logarithmic entropy exponent follow. The text correctly avoids the false multiplicative asymptote `k ~ exp(-9 sigma^2)`, does not model event counts as exactly Poisson, and distinguishes comparing two rare-rate alternatives from merely comparing against the tree.

**Unfolding.** The perturbed source changes input heights but not the modal flow or output wedge. The unchanged slow residues preserve the disconnected-support exhaustion; the fast singleton acquires exits `(z+delta,z-delta)`. The exact discriminant, nonzero root derivative, and uniform strict reduction/construction margins are enough for the stated conventional local proof. Equality still pairs each input/output zero and the two tangent-face zeros into reciprocal missing edges. The existence argument is geometric and uses strict remaining margins, rather than inference from floating-point support zeros. Entropy separation is obtained by continuity from the certified positive gap at `delta=0`; the text explicitly restricts that consequence to a possibly smaller neighborhood instead of promoting the full rectangle's geometry certificate to an entropy-gap certificate.

**Statistics and conditioning.** The full Jacobian includes the derivative of the mark-rate matrix `B`, so observed rates are genuinely estimated. Its rank at a tree follows by differentiating the rational left inverse and taking an interior limit. The fixed-`e` log-coordinate scaling is analytic, not a fitted power law. For finite records the centered row features are martingale differences with respect to the interval filtration: the next incoming mark is known, and its resolved endpoint resets the physical state. Cross-time covariance vanishes; disjoint current-row indicators give zero cross-row covariance. Random row counts enter by the usual ratio-estimator limit and do not justify an iid conditional-on-counts assertion. The manuscript correctly uses the former reasoning. First-derivative entropy degeneracy at equilibrium, missing intervals, conditional coverage, boundary fits, count-warning heuristics, and numerical rate floors are disclosed.

**Regularization.** The rate-floor/activity example has normalized stationary law `(1/(c+2),(2c+1)/(3(c+2)),1/3)`, activity below three, and entropy diverging as `(log c)/3`. It therefore refutes an invalid interchange of rate floor, rate ceiling, and affinity information. Fixed-support compactness smooths the generator entropy functional; it does not remove an entropy-varying observation fiber. The bounded-ratio-cone continuity/Lipschitz argument correctly treats vanishing paired rates using directed rate-ratio entropy terms and the smooth stationary vector near an irreducible limit. The compact-quotient conclusion is conditional on entropy being identifiable **throughout** the compact class. Local detailed balance and channel resolution are treated as additional physical assumptions, rather than facts inferred from an abstract generator.
