\subsection{Compact physical priors and shrinking data tolerance}
\label{subsec:compact-priors}
Fix a state cap $\bar n\ge2$, the named microscopic endpoints of the
resolved reverse pair, and $0<m<M<\infty$. Let $\mathcal C_{\rm reg}$ contain the
connected reciprocal generators with $2\le N\le\bar n$ for which every
supported rate belongs to $[m,M]$. Absent reciprocal pairs are allowed;
the observed pair is present with its declared incidence. The values of
its rates may be unknown. Equip this class with the finite disjoint-union
topology over dimensions and the usual matrix topology within each
dimension. Hidden-state relabeling does not change the conclusions.

\begin{proposition}[Attained upper bounds under compact priors]
\label{prop:compact-priors}
The class $\mathcal C_{\rm reg}$ is compact, its joint waiting-law map is continuous
in maximum row total variation, and its stationary entropy functional is
continuous. For feasible exact data $D\in\Obs(\mathcal C_{\rm reg})$ define
\begin{equation}
 U_D(\eta)=\max\{\sig(Q):Q\in\mathcal C_{\rm reg},
                      d(\Obs(Q),D)\le\eta\},\qquad\eta\ge0.
 \label{eq:compact-upper}
\end{equation}
Every maximum exists and is finite, and
\begin{equation}
 U_D(\eta)\downarrow U_D(0)\qquad(\eta\downarrow0).
 \label{eq:compact-limit}
\end{equation}
The assertion also holds with a metric generating weak convergence of
the joint row laws in place of $d$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For each fixed dimension and connected reciprocal support, supported
ordered rates range over a closed finite box and diagonals are fixed by
row sums. There are only finitely many such supports and dimensions.
Their union is compact. In a converging fixed-dimensional sequence,
each off-diagonal lies in $\{0\}\cup[m,M]$, so its support is eventually
constant. The limit retains a connected support and is irreducible.

Here is an explicit common waiting-time tail. Set
\begin{equation}
 \nu=(\bar n-1)M,\qquad t_0=1/\nu,\qquad
 b=\frac{e^{-1}}{\bar n!}\left(\frac m\nu\right)^{\bar n}>0.
\end{equation}
Append an absorbing state reached at any observed jump and uniformize
at rate $\nu$. From every state, a path to absorption uses at most
$\bar n$ transitions, each with uniformized probability at least
$m/\nu$. Absorption earlier on the path also counts. The event of
exactly $\bar n$ Poisson steps in time $t_0$ therefore gives absorption
probability at least $b$. Repeating the bound by the Markov property,
\begin{equation}
 \sup_{Q\in\mathcal C_{\rm reg},i}
 \Pr_i^Q\{\tau_{\rm mark}>t\}
 \le(1-b)^{\lfloor t/t_0\rfloor}.
 \label{eq:compact-tail}
\end{equation}
On a finite time interval, continuity of the matrix exponential gives
uniform convergence of $Re^{T_Qt}B_Q$. Equation~\eqref{eq:compact-tail}
controls the discarded probability tails uniformly. Splitting the
row-TV integral at a large finite time proves continuity of $\Obs$.
The normalized stationary solution and the entropy sum are continuous
on each support box, because the stationary distribution and supported
rates stay positive. Finite union gives continuity on $\mathcal C_{\rm reg}$.

The constraint set in Eq.~\eqref{eq:compact-upper} is nonempty and
compact, so its maximum is attained. These sets shrink with $\eta$,
and their maxima are bounded below by $U_D(0)$. For any
$\eta_j\downarrow0$, extract a convergent subsequence of maximizers.
Its limit belongs to the exact fiber by observation continuity; entropy
continuity makes the limiting value at most $U_D(0)$. This proves
Eq.~\eqref{eq:compact-limit}. Row-TV continuity also implies weak
continuity, so the same compactness argument applies to a metric for
weak convergence.
\end{proof}

An explicit data-independent bound follows by setting $A=\log(M/m)$.
Microscopic activity is at most $(\bar n-1)M$, so the established
activity--affinity bound~\cite{NishiyamaHasegawa2023} gives
\begin{equation}
 U_D(\eta)\le(\bar n-1)M A\tanh(A/2).
\end{equation}
The prior bounds make the constrained upper value finite; they do not
make the compatible generator or its entropy unique. The proposition
holds the feasible center $D$ fixed and shrinks its tolerance. It does
not assert two-sided continuity as $D$ varies, good inverse conditioning,
or a global certificate for a numerical optimizer. Closed tolerance
balls are used for attainment; an open ball may have only a supremum.

Finally, this deterministic statement is not a confidence-set
construction. Raw empirical waiting laws are atomic and have row-TV
distance one from the continuous model laws. A practical use therefore
requires a justified fitted-density TV region or a weaker metric or
measurement region with its own statistical coverage. The rate floor,
rate ceiling and state cap remain explicit physical assumptions.
