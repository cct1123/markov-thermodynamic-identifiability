## Narrow manuscript wording

The following language states the contribution without a priority claim:

> Compatible hidden-model optimization and numerical evidence for apparently unbounded compatible entropy were already developed for masked dynamics. We ask for exact upper-range and local upper-ceiling statements in the declared continuous-time, resolved-edge model classes. With a finite state cap and a fixed positive interval for every supported rate, the class is compact despite unknown support. This yields an attained data-compatible entropy maximum and convergence to the exact-fiber maximum as a closed observational tolerance shrinks. The latter is a compactness consequence, distinct from the established microscopic activity–affinity bound and from statistical coverage of an estimated data region.

For the known-network issue, a short separate sentence is sufficient:

> One observed reverse pair already suffices for total entropy in a unicyclic network; the obstruction studied here concerns larger compatibility classes that do not impose that topology.

Use the existing Ehrich/Nitzan citations for compatible optimization, Nishiyama–Hasegawa for the sharp prior upper bound, Fritz–Ertel–Seifert for finite observation, and both 2022 transition papers for the single-edge and killed-process antecedents. The new compactness result should not be advertised as a novel maximum theorem or as an algorithm for certifying the global maximum.

## Boundaries on the comparison

These primary reads do not establish that no matching earlier compact-prior proposition exists. Compactness, continuity of finite CTMCs under uniform irreducibility, and nested compact maximization are elementary standard mechanisms. The justified added value is making the physical assumptions, support/dimension union, joint-law metric, exact-zero-tolerance fiber and inference limitations explicit in this manuscript. The global five-state claim is being audited separately and is not endorsed by this attribution note.
