## Scientific positioning

The central contribution to state is the **complete physical entropy identified set for an explicit five-state family, combined with the exact dimension/support criterion for a finite local entropy upper ceiling under a single resolved transition pair**. These are model-specific quantified statements. Similarity descriptions of equivalent positive models, isolated finite factorization alternatives, hidden equilibrium ambiguity, and divergence at vanishing reverse rates are established antecedents.

Suggested manuscript language: “We determine the entire entropy-production fiber of an explicit five-state family, allowing unknown bidirected support, and establish a necessary-and-sufficient local upper-ceiling criterion at each fixed state count. The first result requires a global exhaustion of physical realizations; the second distinguishes instability of the entropy functional from ambiguity of the generator.” This asserts the proved results without a literature-wide priority claim.

The closest physical antecedent is Ehrich's exact compatible-model optimization under a discrete-time masked observation. The closest mathematical antecedents are the complete similarity-and-positivity equivalence description of Vanluyten et al., the finite static factorization example of Mond et al., and the invariant PH polytopes of He and Zhang. None of their inspected statements supplies the project's prescribed two-reset CTMC fiber count, the two certified stationary entropy values, or the all-N local-ceiling quantifiers. This hypothesis comparison supports the distinction; it does not prove that no earlier matching result exists.

Avoid claims of the first finite positive-realization fiber, first use of invariant cones for PH laws, first hidden entropy ambiguity, a new reverse-Pinsker failure, or a new generic confidence-set impossibility theorem. The three-state divergence is an informative constrained example of an established singularity, not a sufficient headline contribution by itself.

## Residual novelty and source-access risks

The earlier audits examined further neighboring work on aggregated CTMCs, MAP/BMAP canonical forms, Coxian representations, inverse-Jacobi theory, NMF rigidity, and HMM inverse stability. Those full distinctions remain in the [realization comparison](../evidence/publication-realization-novelty.md) and [thermodynamic comparison](../evidence/publication-thermodynamics-novelty.md). They need not all be cited in a focused introduction. Larget (1998), Kienker (1989), and Förster–Nagy (2000) retain exact-theorem access gaps; no claim of exhaustive novelty follows. A matching physical-realization theorem, especially one with an exceptional finite whole fiber, would alter attribution. No new contrary theorem was found in this pass, whose purpose was verification of the manuscript's actual citations.

The strongest defensible comparison is therefore affirmative: explicitly identify the physical restrictions and prove the count and ceiling criterion. Avoid blanket statements that prior inference methods cannot reconstruct entropy, since the cited transition and cycle results do so under their own assumptions. Unknown topology, a state cap, a prescribed state count, linear minimality, and chosen canonical topology are distinct restrictions.

## Journal and current instructions

**Recommendation: Physical Review E, Regular Article, Statistical Physics section.** The current [scope](https://journals.aps.org/pre/about) includes statistical physics and detailed research articles. The [section guide](https://journals.aps.org/authors/guidelines-section-selection-physical-review-e) explicitly includes stochastic processes and thermodynamics. This is a scope assessment, not a prediction of acceptance. A Letter would compress proof material essential for evaluating the central claim.

The [current author instructions](https://journals.aps.org/pre/authors), retrieved 2026-09-11, allow regular articles without a length limit, prefer LaTeX source with REVTeX templates, encourage titles in references, require an understandable standalone paper, and require a data-availability statement. Essential proofs should be appendices; supplementary files can hold executable checks and extended output. Figures should remain readable without color alone. These points justify `revtex4-2` with `aps,pre,preprint` for a readable expert-review manuscript. The [REVTeX page](https://journals.aps.org/revtex) identifies 4.2f as current; the preprint layout is an editorial choice, not a journal mandate.

The [APS AI policy](https://journals.aps.org/authors/appropriate-use-ai-tools), “Authors/Disclosure,” requires substantive AI use to be disclosed and research use to be described in methods. A disclosure must accurately identify tool/model versions, their roles, and the checks actually performed. It must not assert that a human has reviewed the science when that review remains pending. The AI assistant cannot be listed as an author. The package can be ready for expert human review while human authorship/affiliation, final accountability and public repository details remain to be completed before submission.

Suggested factual draft disclosure: “OpenAI Codex was used to assist literature retrieval, derivations, code preparation, independent computational checks and manuscript drafting. The reproducibility package identifies the executed checks and their outputs.” Add the exact confirmed model/version and the responsible human's review statement at the final human submission stage; do not invent either. A data-availability statement should describe the actual included package, not claim an uncreated public DOI or repository deposit.
