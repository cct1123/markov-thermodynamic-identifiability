## Counterexamples and statistical meaning

The proposed leaf tuples, in order **(r,s,a,b,c,d)**, give identical full matrices at lambda=1 and 2. At 3 the lower-left entries are 4/19 and 10/47. They differ in r, trace and absolute stationary event intensities (1/3 versus 6/17 per mark); normalized mark fractions agree. Both have zero entropy. This refutes two-point generator identification at an uncalibrated leaf, not full-law or entropy identification. One-point insufficiency for complete triangles is also checked exactly. [Results and domain exclusions](outputs/correctness-2026-09-15/laplace-two.json).

The manuscript now explicitly states that a finite atomic empirical waiting row has TV distance one from the continuous model law. The operational estimator uses bounded Laplace moments instead: stationary CTMC trajectories, initial censored interval discarded, fixed n complete intervals, constrained positive-rate fitting, and a dependence-aware sandwich covariance. Existing 900-trajectory results remain separately labeled empirical evidence. The rigorous new confidence rectangle uses no stationarity assumption for the initial mark, but does require resolved reset events and the stated fixed-n design. Its entropy upper endpoint can be infinite. Same-record adaptive arguments, fixed-window terminal censoring and missed events need separate analysis.

## Contribution, assumptions and remaining uncertainty

The defensible contribution is a support-exhaustive CTMC example with exactly two endpoint entropy values and a unique generator above, together with the separation between exact identification and local upper inference. Compatible hidden-model optimization and numerical evidence for unbounded compatible dissipation precede this work (Ehrich); tolerance-constrained fitting also precedes it (Nitzan et al.). Nishiyama–Hasegawa's sharp microscopic activity/rate-ratio bound is credited with its counting convention and units. Fritz–Ertel–Seifert's finite temporal bins retain every event and its order; their analysis is not a lost-event model or a coverage proof for our estimator. Known-unicyclic single-edge entropy recovery also predates this work. [Inspected primary sources and precise locators](analysis/correctness/PRIMARY-COMPARISON.md).

Application requires a time-homogeneous finite irreducible CTMC, known microscopic endpoints and individually resolved reverse transitions, reciprocal support, declared full joint waiting laws and absolute time scale, and the stated physical dimension restriction. Minimal observable order is a lower bound, not a physical cap. Graph entropy becomes physical dissipation only under appropriate even-state/channel resolution and local detailed balance; unresolved parallel channels can hide further dissipation. Physical rate floors/caps are external information.

No unresolved theorem-level gap was found in this targeted revision after the repairs. Remaining limits are the unclassified full subcritical range, arbitrary larger-network fibers/observation graphs, broader perturbation genericity, optimal rare-event estimation and detector models. Conventional proof and exact-script encoding still warrant expert review. Earlier inaccessible or partially read realization sources remain attribution limitations; no literature-wide novelty or formal-verification claim is made.
