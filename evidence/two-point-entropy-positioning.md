## Thermodynamic comparison

**Primary result, reread:** Jannik Ehrich, *Tightest bound on hidden entropy production from partially observed dynamics*, inspected [arXiv:2105.08803v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2105.08803v2), Section 4.1, retrieved 2026-09-10. Its sampled maximum over a four-state discrete-time aggregate-equivalence class grows near a one-way boundary. The source presents this as apparent unboundedness from the explored grid. The original report and its evidence status remain [E004](RECORDS.md#e004) and [E025](RECORDS.md#e025).

**Inference:** neither the divergent boundary mechanism nor growing entropy within an observation class is new. The repository's exact constructions strengthen their own specific model claims to proofs; they do not retroactively turn the source's numerical exploration into a proof or equate aggregation with resolved-edge data.

**Primary result:** Julius Fritz, Benjamin Ertel and Udo Seifert, *Entropy estimation for partially accessible Markov networks based on imperfect observations: Role of finite resolution and finite statistics*, Physical Review E 111, 044106 (2025), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.111.044106); inspected [arXiv:2412.04102v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.04102v2), 8 April 2025, retrieved 2026-09-10. Sections II.2, III and VI analyze resolution changes and empirical fluctuations of lower entropy estimators. Section III explicitly allows finite-sample estimates to exceed their asymptotic bounds.

**Inference:** this is close finite-data prior art, but its inspected claims do not give an impossibility theorem for uniformly valid finite upper confidence limits over all compatible microscopic models. Our theorem's model class and uniform coverage quantifiers must be stated rather than describing all finite-data entropy estimation as impossible.

**Previously inspected primary result, implication retained:** Tomohiro Nishiyama and Yoshihiko Hasegawa, *Upper bound for entropy production in Markov processes*, inspected [arXiv:2306.15251v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.15251v2), 6 October 2023, Eq. (8), as recorded in [E037](RECORDS.md#e037). The bound uses maximal microscopic rate ratio as well as activity. Our bounded-rate examples have unbounded rate ratios, so they do not violate that theorem. This run rechecked the primary bibliographic record but did not independently rederive that source's bound.

## Statistical mechanism

**Primary result:** Jonas Moss, *Infinite diameter confidence sets in Hedges' publication bias model*, Journal of the Korean Statistical Society 51, 946–960 (2022), [full publisher text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42952-022-00169-1), published 22 April 2022, retrieved 2026-09-10. The coordinator and independent verifier read Section 3, Theorem 4, and its appendix proof. The theorem combines converging distributions and diverging parameter size, with a support condition for conclusions at other distributions.

**Inference:** our direct TV-transfer proof uses established confidence-set impossibility reasoning. It indexes coverage by microscopic models because distinct entropy values may share an observation law. We do not import a theorem assuming an identified functional without checking that issue. The [independent note](../analysis/finite-precision-independent-check.md#primary-statistical-analogy-and-source-limits) records the older Gleser–Hwang and Bahadur–Savage original-source access gaps. Their original papers were not read.

## What this supports and what could change it

The defensible research objects are the exact two-point **physical entropy** fiber, its sharp boundary, and explicit fixed-cap/bounded-rate instability results with their observation and coverage assumptions. Finiteness of realization fibers, normalized similarities, near-one-way dissipation and generic confidence-set impossibility are established ideas.

A primary result with the same fixed-order bidirected resolved-edge class and the same entire-fiber or local-instability conclusion would materially reduce the proposed distinction. None was located in the bounded comparison, which does not establish absence. Untargeted searches for finite/isolated positive realizations and entropy non-estimability mostly returned already-separated models or irrelevant information-theoretic entropy. A 2026 stochastic-irreversibility review lead (Ghosal–Bisker, DOI 10.1039/d5cp04712a) returned a PMC browser check and failed publisher HTML; no uninspected theorem from it is used.

The unresolved novelty risk is specifically a prior theorem/example with the same physical restrictions, not a lack of general mathematical antecedents. The source comparisons narrow a potential publication claim but cannot certify it.
