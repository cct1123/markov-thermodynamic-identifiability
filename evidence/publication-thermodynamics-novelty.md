## What the new search changes

The three-state divergence alone is not a persuasive originality claim. Vanishing reverse-rate entropy singularities and the impossibility of controlling KL divergence by TV alone are established. Fixing trace and maximum rate makes a clean minimal example, but does not create a new singularity mechanism. The stronger contribution is the physical realization problem: an exhausted finite entropy fiber (T3), and a necessary-and-sufficient local-ceiling criterion for one resolved pair at every fixed state count (T6). These remain candidate contributions after theorem comparison; a finite search cannot certify originality.

## Closest additional primary results

**Zeraati, Jafarpour and Hinrichsen (2012).** *Entropy Production of Nonequilibrium Steady States with Irreversible Transitions*, J. Stat. Mech. L12001, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-5468/2012/12/L12001), [full arXiv v1 including supplement](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.4701), 20 November 2012. Read main introduction/Eq. (1), finite-observation argument and supplement Section 3, especially Eqs. (23)–(32). The source explicitly treats logarithmic divergence at a vanishing backward rate and a Bayesian finite-observation regularization. The supplement distinguishes averaging a logarithm from taking the logarithm of an average. **Difference:** T4a keeps every model bidirected, both new chord rates vanish, the limiting source remains irreducible and exactly identifiable, and trace/order/rates stay controlled. Those constraints are a specific constructive strengthening, not a new entropy singularity. The failed HTML-v2 route was replaced by the accessible v1 PDF; no v2 text was inferred.

**Busiello, Gupta and Maritan (2020).** *Entropy production in systems with unidirectional transitions*, Phys. Rev. Research **2**, 023011, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.2.023011), [full institutional journal PDF](https://www.research.unipd.it/retrieve/7b040115-3663-423d-bf76-d8ab6426b45d/PhysRevResearch.2.023011.pdf). Read Sections I–III, Eqs. (8)–(13), and bibliography [19]. Their dynamics/flux-preserving construction defines an alternative finite contribution for genuinely unidirectional transitions by a limit with a fictitious reverse rate tending to infinity. **Difference:** the repository uses ordinary stationary entropy only on reciprocal finite-rate models and takes a reverse rate to zero; it does not assign a regularized value at a one-way boundary. This primary forward citation of Zeraati prevents conflating two entropy conventions.

**Sason and Verdú (2015).** *Upper Bounds on the Relative Entropy and Rényi Divergence as a Function of Total Variation Distance for Finite Alphabets*, IEEE ITW–Fall, pp. 214–218, [full author PDF](https://webee.technion.ac.il/people/sason/ISSV-ITW15-Korea-paper.pdf). Read introduction, Theorem 1/Eqs. (10)–(11), proof and references [6], [8], [20]. The paper states that arbitrarily small TV can coexist with arbitrarily large KL and gives reverse bounds requiring a positive reference mass. **Difference:** that general fact already explains the entropy sensitivity of rare reverse fluxes; it does not impose CTMC stationarity, trace or exact observed fibers. T4 realizes this familiar mechanism under those constraints. Failure of a general reverse Pinsker inequality must not be advertised as a project discovery.

**Sason and Verdú (2016).** *f-Divergence Inequalities*, IEEE Trans. Information Theory **62**, 5973–6006, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2016.2603151), [full author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1508.00335), draft dated 6 December 2016. Read Section VI, Theorems 26–28, Remarks 27–35 and their proof passages. Bounds use relative-information limits, Lipschitz restrictions or minimum reference masses. This is the actual forward development referenced by the 2015 paper, with numbering different from its early preprint. **Difference:** T8 is a straightforward regularity principle of this kind, not a new general information inequality; T5/T6 establish which physical observation classes defeat an upper ceiling even at fixed trace.

**van Erven and Harremoës (2014).** *Rényi Divergence and Kullback–Leibler Divergence*, IEEE Trans. Information Theory **60**, 3797–3820, [full preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1206.2459). Read Section III.D, Theorems 15–17 and proofs. Positive-order divergence is lower semicontinuous in the pair of laws under setwise convergence; the stated uniform-TV continuity result concerns orders strictly between zero and one. **Difference:** upward KL/entropy divergence at a paired-zero boundary does not contradict lower semicontinuity. T4 is not a claim of downward discontinuity or a new general topology of divergence. The search lead referring to “Theorem 10” was checked and rejected as a locator: in this version that theorem concerns finite partitions, not continuity.

**Baiesi, Nishiyama and Falasco (2024).** *Effective estimation of entropy production with lacking data*, Communications Physics **7**, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-024-01742-2), [full arXiv v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.04657v3). Read Introduction, empirical-estimation and hyperaccurate-current sections, Eqs. (2), (3), (15)–(19), bibliography [35]. Their result is a lower bound; an empirical unidirectional regime uses an assumption about total unobserved reverse flux of order inverse observation time. **Difference:** T7 rules out uniformly finite upper confidence limits without such extra restrictions. It neither contradicts nor replaces this lower estimator. This is a verified forward citation of Zeraati. The Nature direct page failed before full preprint access succeeded; only the version actually read is used.

**Nishiyama and Hasegawa (2023).** *Upper bound for entropy production in Markov processes*, Phys. Rev. E **108**, 044139, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.108.044139), [full publisher PDF](https://harvest.aps.org/v2/journals/articles/10.1103/PhysRevE.108.044139/fulltext). Re-read Section II.A Eqs. (6)–(8), Appendix A and concluding discussion. The steady bound is sigma<=A log(R)(R-1)/(R+1), where R is the maximum reciprocal rate ratio. The authors explicitly note that partial observation can leave R inaccessible. **Difference:** trace or activity caps do not bound R. T5 supplies a same-count, fixed-trace sequence defeating an observation-only upper ceiling; T8's elementary ratio bound is weaker than this known inequality and must be credited accordingly.

**Biddle (2026).** *A method for calculating entropy production without knowing transition rates or dwell times*, [full arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.27635v1), 26 May 2026; [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physa.2026.131934), Physica A **700**, 131934, issue dated 15 October 2026. The issue date is future relative to this retrieval, while the preprint and publisher page are already accessible. Read Sections 2–4.2 and Eqs. (10)–(16). Cycle-family forward/reverse counts and chord currents reconstruct entropy, with hidden-state extensions when the needed cycle families and chords remain observable. Total trajectory duration supplies the time scale. **Difference:** a single resolved pair with unknown hidden topology need not supply those cycle observables. Exact identification conditional on that graph/data structure does not count T3's entire physical fiber or prove a uniform upper bound near it. The title alone would have obscured these assumptions.

## Backward and forward tracing; remaining limits

The inspected Zeraati references point backward to Schnakenberg (1976), Andrieux–Gaspard and Seifert's trajectory formulation. The Schnakenberg [publisher record](https://journals.aps.org/rmp/abstract/10.1103/RevModPhys.48.571) was accessible, but its PDF fetch failed. No fresh full-original proof read is claimed. Busiello [19] and Baiesi [35] are actual inspected forward references. The 2015 information paper points backward to Csiszár–Talata (2006), Lemma 6.3/p. 1012, and van Erven–Harremoës; the former remains a secondary attribution here, while the latter was read. The 2016 information paper supplies an inspected forward theorem, not a guessed citation relationship.

The July 2026 [Seifert review's publisher bibliography](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42254-026-00954-5) was used only to locate current primary work and verify forward references to the previously read 2022–2025 resolved-transition papers and Nitzan–Ghosal–Bisker (2023). It is a secondary review, not evidence that no upper-bound theorem exists. Its abstract/references, not the paywalled body, were available through that route. The newly found Biddle primary statement was then read directly. Generic “identified set,” “observational equivalence,” “thermodynamic stability,” “locally bounded entropy production,” and bounded-rate inverse searches returned much unrelated material; that is a search limitation, not novelty evidence.

## Readiness judgment

**Verdict 1: ready for manuscript preparation**, confined to T3 and T6 with their necessary lemmas and sharp counterexamples. This means the proved contribution is substantial enough to assemble and submit to expert scrutiny; it is not certification of novelty or journal acceptance. The combination answers two exact questions: what the entire entropy identified set can be, and when any finite upper ceiling survives observational tolerance. The three-state example demonstrates that failure of the second can occur despite a well-behaved generator inverse.

The strongest novelty risk is a prior physical-realization classification with the same exceptional count or local-ceiling quantifiers, especially in the specifically inaccessible originals catalogued by the realization audit. Larget's original remains a documented gap after genuinely new routes; canonical-form uniqueness alone is not evidence of anticipation. A matching construction/theorem would change attribution and possibly the verdict. An expert objection to the global endpoint exhaustion would change mathematical status. Neither gap is repaired by more untargeted searches. The next useful action is a self-contained theorem-and-proof manuscript core, with the exact comparison table available for expert review; no external communication is authorized by this note.
