## Corrections exposed during mapping

No new mathematical contradiction or missing main report claim was found. Four representation errors were corrected before acceptance: a saved-manifest check no longer carries an independent-science badge; physical context is not presented as logically necessary for every algebraic lemma; exact counterexamples are distinct from falsified proposals; and numerical verification edges state that they cover examples/identities rather than universal quantifiers. The complete independent review is [semantic-review.md](semantic-review.md).

The mapping also clarified known boundaries: fixed N is not a cap on N; a fixed trace is not stationary activity; a sufficient trace-derived cap is not every tighter cap; generator-entry and observational neighborhoods differ; the cap-free joint-kernel proof does not automatically transfer to stationary finite windows. These are existing scientific distinctions, not new discoveries by visualization.

The initial root summary of the balanced numerical search called its two settings “models.” The saved output instead records two positivity floors, 1e-5 and 1e-7, with 26 total starts. The final node preserves the actual floors, z/coordinate bounds, determinant floor, seed, iteration limit and tolerance. The correction does not affect any theorem: the search is discovery only.
