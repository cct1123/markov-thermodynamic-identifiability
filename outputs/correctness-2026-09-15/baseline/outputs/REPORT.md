# Research synthesis: thermodynamic identifiability and unstable upper inference

14 September 2026. The strongest supported answer is that **exact recovery of partially observed Markov dynamics does not guarantee stable or bounded inference of microscopic entropy production**. With one resolved microscopic reverse pair, unknown reciprocal support and exactly N states, a finite local entropy upper ceiling exists precisely for N=2 or a complete N=3 source. This criterion remains valid with observed rates and trace fixed; arbitrary further support or tight-rate constraints can change the model class. [E061](../evidence/RECORDS.md#e061).

The complete deliverable is the [revised paper](../manuscript/main.pdf), its [proof appendices](../manuscript/supplementary/proofs.tex), [revision notes](../REVISION_NOTES.md) and [reproducible package](manuscript-package.zip). The preceding report is preserved in [revision/baseline/REPORT.md](revision/baseline/REPORT.md). This synthesis covers the expanded article, not a general classification of every small network.

## What is established

Three distinct positive Laplace arguments identify every irreducible three-state generator under a three-state cap. The raw inverse is locally Lipschitz even at a tree. Its relative-rate conditioning and the entropy functional have different behavior: for a fixed positive entrance and small reverse k, the log-rate condition number grows as 1/k. The full-six-rate derivative includes changes in the observed jump amplitudes, not just the killed resolvent. The numerical design comparison shows that maximizing a Euclidean singular value and minimizing entropy uncertainty can select different Laplace triples. [E060](../evidence/RECORDS.md#e060), [E065](../evidence/RECORDS.md#e065).

The trace-minus-four three-state family has rates at most one and k=exp(-1/e²), while sigma is asymptotic to 1/(3e). It converges entrywise and in observed waiting laws to an irreducible tree. The singularity is thus partly in the entropy functional itself. An upper rate bound controls timescales but does not control the logarithm of a vanishing reverse rate. [E057](../evidence/RECORDS.md#e057), [E061](../evidence/RECORDS.md#e061).

For fixed p>1, the admissible alternatives Q(e,k) and Q(e,k^p) have asymptotically p-fold different entropy. A test of their stationary records with error sum at most gamma<1 requires H≥(1-gamma)/(k-k^p)-1, even with full microscopic observations. The logarithm of this bound divided by the smaller entropy squared tends to 9. This is a lower bound for a specified testing problem, not a universal achievable inference rate. If k were tied to e by a known functional prior for every competitor, the second alternative would be excluded. [E066](../evidence/RECORDS.md#e066).

At least two states touching no observed transition suffice for unbounded entropy in every neighborhood under unknown reciprocal support, preserving state count, observed rates and trace. Conversely, if observed reciprocal edges touch every vertex in a known state set, a selected square kernel resolvent recovers the full generator from two Laplace points (one when observed rates are known). This is a partial graph classification; one uncovered vertex remains unresolved in general. Even full vertex coverage cannot cure entropy discontinuity when a previously absent unobserved pair may open. [E068](../evidence/RECORDS.md#e068).

The balanced five-state source retains the exact global classification: unbounded entropy below z*, exactly two distinct finite values at z*, and a unique generator above, under a five-state cap. The new exit-asymmetry perturbation has an exact discriminant polynomial and a local critical curve with derivative about .4650042 at zero. Exact interval signs certify the geometric classification near the original point; entropy separation persists in a possibly smaller neighborhood. A wider 41-node continuation is numerical evidence. The fold concerns active boundary equations; the full positive fiber has a continuum below the boundary. Adding a sixth state permits divergent exact-law splitting. [E059](../evidence/RECORDS.md#e059), [E067](../evidence/RECORDS.md#e067).

## Finite-data implications and physical restrictions

The constructive estimator uses empirical joint Laplace transforms from actual marked CTMC records, fits all six rates and propagates moment uncertainty through stationary entropy. A bounded-martingale argument supplies pointwise asymptotic covariance without falsely assuming independent successive mark rows. The 900-run study reports all estimates and failures. At an interior example 5,000 events yield 8.8% relative entropy RMSE; at 20,000 the relative RMSE is 4.6%. A rare-reverse example still has frequent boundary/rank failures and extreme undercoverage. Small observable residuals are not a certificate of accurate entropy. Numerical rate bounds and the suggested rare-count warning are explicit restrictions, not data-derived physical priors. [E065](../evidence/RECORDS.md#e065).

A published sharp stationary bound controls network entropy by microscopic activity K and maximum log rate affinity a_max: sigma≤K a_max tanh(a_max/2). Neither affinity alone nor a rate ceiling alone suffices. An exact three-state example further shows that a positive rate floor plus bounded activity need not suffice. Fixed-support compactness or bounded affinities regularizes the generator entropy map but does not automatically identify an entropy-varying observation fiber or make its inverse well conditioned. [E068](../evidence/RECORDS.md#e068).

Physical heat dissipation additionally requires correct state parity, reservoir/channel resolution and local detailed balance. A mathematical CTMC need not specify unique physical heat currents. Stationarity, absolute timing, known event incidence, finite-state Markov dynamics and the detector model are consequential assumptions. Binned times, missed events, blurred labels and detector memory are different changes of experiment.

## Confidence, contrary explanations and verification

Confidence is strong in the specified conventional derivations and their exact algebraic/interval checks; the broad novelty assessment remains provisional. All original theorem statements and proof appendices are preserved, and all 15 historical replay payloads match. Twenty-two accepted JSON outputs remain unchanged. New tests use alternative derivatives, time-domain integrals, stationary flux sums and independent endpoint coordinates. Numerical continuation is not proof of global exclusion, and Monte Carlo coverage is not a uniform guarantee. [Theorem audit](../analysis/revision/AUDIT.md), [reproduction manifest](revision/reproduction.json). The final 45-page PDF was visually reviewed; a fresh extraction passed the entire workflow and rebuilt the identical PDF. [E070](../evidence/RECORDS.md#e070).

The strongest counterexplanations have been tested or made explicit: fixed rate-relation priors remove the hard rare alternatives; fixed support can remove entropy singularities; tight caps can collapse the competitor class; an extra hidden dimension changes the exact fiber; an active-boundary fold does not count the full continuum; lower-bound saturation under a selected topology does not determine an upper bound over broader competitors. Source comparisons include closely related waiting-time lower estimators, finite-statistics work, hidden entropy, MAP/PH/HMM and aggregated realizations. Some older originals remain inaccessible. [E069](../evidence/RECORDS.md#e069), [literature ledger](../analysis/revision/LITERATURE.md).

## Remaining uncertainty and stopping

The revised manuscript answers the scoped request with strengthened proofs, explicit counterexamples, reproducible experiments, source comparisons and limitations. No additional ordinary parameter sweep would resolve the remaining expert-proof and attribution risk. The next valuable review is a human check of the global invariant-triangle exhaustion and the exact margins transferred to the local unfolding.

Further scientific progress would require a new discriminating question: classify a one-uncovered-state graph, certify an explicit off-balanced entropy-gap interval, optimize statistically efficient rare-rate inference under stated priors, or specify an actual detector model and compare its likelihood against the perfect-record estimator. Each could change a stated boundary of the current results. General five/six-state fibers, unrestricted dimensions without physical restrictions and cap-free stationary-window positive continuity remain unresolved. They are not silently converted into negative conclusions by failed numerical searches.
