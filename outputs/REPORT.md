## Physical mechanism and usable statistics

Bounded kinetic speed allows diverging logarithmic affinity. In the three-state sparse sequence the current tends to zero as e/3 while the cycle affinity grows as 1/e², producing entropy asymptotic to 1/(3e). This singularity exists even under small full-generator entrywise error. Partial observation additionally permits exact-law ambiguity within complete support: a displayed four-state path has bounded rates, the same full observed kernel and entropy asymptotic to (557/2130) log[1/(2/3-e)]. Its reverse flux vanishes at an excluded one-way boundary. [E075](../evidence/RECORDS.md#e075).

Raw finite empirical waiting laws are atomic and have TV distance one from continuous model laws. The existing operational estimator instead fits bounded Laplace moments from actual sequential CTMC records. Its 900 seeded trials and dependence-aware pointwise covariance remain useful empirical evidence, including severe undercoverage near a rare reverse rate. A separate finite-n martingale argument now proves coverage for a simultaneous moment rectangle with random mark counts and unconstrained empty rows. Inverting that rectangle can give infinite entropy upper limits; a large feasible optimization result is not an upper certificate. Fixed n complete intervals and preselected arguments are essential.
